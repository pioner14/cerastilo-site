Two apps, one database

The Christian Life and Ministry meeting schedule — on your computer and your tablet

Cerastilo is a free scheduler for the Christian Life & Ministry meeting. Two apps: one for your computer (Linux and Windows) and one for your Android tablet. Each works on its own, and together they share one database.

Cerastilo

Two apps, one database

Each app works fully on its own. If you have both a computer and a tablet, they sync over Wi-Fi and always show the same thing.

  • Linux · Windows

    Computer

    Full planning: monthly board, auto-assignment, printing, export, e-mails to participants.

  • Android

    Tablet

    The same app with the same look. Works without a computer — handy to hold right at the meeting.

  • Wi-Fi • SQLite

    Wi-Fi sync

    The tablet acts as the server and the computer connects by itself — with an address and a 6-character code. No firewall setup needed.

    The databases are merged: the more recent edit wins, and a copy is saved before merging.

Need only a computer or only a tablet? Take just one app. Sync is optional.

UI & Workflow

From the workbook to the schedule

Real screenshots of the app (demo data with fictional names). Click an image to open it full size.

Monthly board and auto-assignment

  • Takes qualifications, gender, rest intervals and vacations into account.
  • Fills only empty items — your manual assignments stay.
  • Don’t like the result? Undo with one click.

Today — what to do

  • Who has not replied to a notification and who has not been notified yet.
  • A card for the upcoming meeting.
  • Urgent to-dos on a single screen.

Participants and history

  • Profile cards: qualifications, family, vacation, contacts.
  • Assignment history and “paired N times” — see who has already worked with whom.

Load statistics

  • Charts by assignment category.
  • See at a glance who is overloaded and who has not taken part for a while.

Workbooks

  • Import from EPUB — you download the workbook yourself from its official source.
  • The program is laid out by weeks automatically.

Print and export

  • PDF, Word, S-89 forms, printing.
  • E-mails to participants with a .ics calendar file.
  • The same schedule layout on the computer and on the tablet.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Cerastilo free?

Yes. Cerastilo is free for personal use. The source code can be viewed, but the project is not open source: modifying, redistributing or selling the app is allowed only with the author’s written permission. See the LICENSE file.

What platforms are supported?

Computer: Linux (AUR and x86_64 AppImage) and Windows (x64 ZIP). Android tablets: arm64 APK.

Is my congregation’s data private?

The data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device; the project has no servers of its own. Sync goes directly between your devices over your local Wi-Fi network: the tablet is the server and the computer connects to it. E-mails to participants go through your own SMTP server.

Do I need both devices?

No. Each app works on its own — you can use just the computer or just the tablet. Sync is needed only if you want both devices to hold the same database.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for working with the schedule. The internet is needed only for sending and receiving e-mail.

Can I move assignment history from another program?

There is no direct import from NW Scheduler. Assignment history can be loaded from a CSV file.

Is the UI available in English?

Yes. The interface is available in Russian and English; choose the language in Settings (it applies after a restart).

Does the app include any Jehovah’s Witnesses publications?

No. Cerastilo contains no texts, images or other materials from JW.org and downloads nothing from that site. You download the workbook yourself for personal use and open it in the app. The app lays out the meeting program by weeks only on your own device and does not publish it anywhere. Cerastilo is free and non-commercial.

Is this an official app?

No. Unofficial application. Not affiliated with the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society / JW.org.

Suggest an improvement

I develop Cerastilo on my own. If you are missing a feature or found a bug, tell me what you were trying to do and what happened.

Feature requests and bug reports go through GitHub Issues of the repository. github.com/pioner14/Cerastilo/issues

Support the project

Cerastilo is developed by one person in their spare time. The app is free, but if it helps you, you can support its development. This is entirely optional.

Planned: macOS and iOS. Cerastilo already runs on Linux, Windows, and Android. I also plan to bring it to Mac, iPhone, and iPad, but I need Apple devices to develop and test those versions, and I don’t have them yet. Donations will help me buy the devices and release those versions sooner.
